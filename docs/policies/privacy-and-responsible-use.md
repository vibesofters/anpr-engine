# Privacy and responsible use

The owner reports that the planned public application may accept and process a
real JPEG or PNG following consultation with a lawyer. This is an owner-reported
decision, not an independent legal conclusion by the project agent. It applies
to the bounded public-demonstration design described here and does not approve
unrelated or future image-processing features.

Before upload, the user must receive and explicitly acknowledge a bilingual
notice stating the purpose, their responsibility to be authorized to submit the
image, the possibility of incorrect results, mandatory human review, the
no-intentional-retention policy, the training prohibition, and prohibited uses.
Acknowledgement is a responsible-use control and is not described as the sole
legal basis for processing.

Each request contains one image and is processed only to detect and recognize a
licence plate. Source images, crops, annotations, predictions, corrections, and
results must not be intentionally retained server-side. Submitted material must
not be used for training, evaluation, analytics, debugging archives, dataset
construction, unrelated reuse, or future processing. Operational logs must not
contain image or plate content or other prohibited sensitive fields.

Model output may be wrong even when confident or structurally valid. A person
must confirm, correct, or leave each result unresolved. This research and
demonstration service is not an enforcement or identity system and must not be
used for unlawful tracking, mass surveillance, automated enforcement, identity
inference, or decisions without human review.

Security vulnerabilities must not be submitted through public Issues. Report
security or privacy/data concerns to
[erncnkr@gmail.com](mailto:erncnkr@gmail.com).
