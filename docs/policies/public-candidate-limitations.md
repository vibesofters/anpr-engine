# Public candidate limitations

- No model weights or public model-weight license are provided.
- No real images, plate transcriptions, private datasets, or record-level
  manifests are distributed in the repository.
- Real JPEG/PNG handling is owner-authorized following owner-reported lawyer
  consultation. A bilingual frontend/BFF and private service exist as local
  uncommitted candidates; no deployment exists.
- The local service processes one image per request without intentional
  server-side image/result retention or training use.
- Published metrics are percentage-only development-validation evidence.
- No independent field-holdout, production-accuracy, uptime, or unattended-use
  claim is made.
- Predictions require human confirmation, correction, or unresolved status.
- Infrastructure sizing, staging limits, security, accessibility, native
  Turkish copy, and final release approval remain open.
- Final software licensing remains blocked by dependency, copied/generated code,
  font, dataset, documentation, and compatibility review.
- Barlow Condensed is retained only as a separately licensed SIL OFL 1.1
  synthetic-data dependency; it is not project branding or an official plate
  typeface.
